What we discuss

  • Your goals: visibility, relevant enquiries, professional invitations or clinic development.
  • Your reputation and achievements that can be substantiated.
  • Priority audiences and topics that are useful to them.
  • Media, conferences, websites, social platforms and professional communities.
  • Available time and budget, and the most useful first steps.

How it works

  1. Before the meeting. You share a short questionnaire and links. I review your public presence.
  2. During the meeting. We spend 90–120 minutes discussing your brief and selecting suitable directions.
  3. After the meeting. You receive written recommendations and a 90-day action plan.

Every plan is tailored to the client. Content production, social media management, event organisation and ongoing support are scoped separately. Publications and invitations depend on editors and event organisers.

Preparation for a specific interview or talk

When your plan calls for an interview, camera appearance or conference talk, practical preparation can be arranged separately through the Media Doctor service.
